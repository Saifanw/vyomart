Every product gets a score out of 100. It is our internal screening tool, not a promise of sales. Products scoring 80 or more go to sample testing.

  • A fair selling price compared with Amazon and Flipkart

  • Clear import and compliance status (BIS, EPR where applicable)
  • Warranty and spare parts available in India
  • Positive profit after all costs
